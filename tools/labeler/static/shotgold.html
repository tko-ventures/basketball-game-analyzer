Shot gold: how to label

26 more shots from the four gold games (18 dev, 8 held-out), so that a change to the ball and possession layer can be judged on more than 16 shot attempts and 100 frames. Three tabs, in order. Every answer is saved at once to data/labels/shot_gold.json; you can stop and come back at any point.

Tab 1 · Shot attempts (26 shots, about 20 minutes)

For each shot, check that the list on the right holds every shot attempt and that each one is right. The first pass has already proposed them; fix what is wrong.

  1. Watch the shot: Space plays, ← → step 0.1 s, with Shift 0.5 s. Click the timeline to jump.
  2. Release: go to the first frame where the ball has left the shooter's hands and press S.
  3. Rim: go to the frame where the ball reaches the basket and press R.
  4. Outcome and points: M made or X missed; 2 or 3 points.
  5. Shooter: G jumps to the release frame; click the shooter's box there. If the shooter has no box, drag one around the whole player. T switches the team.
  6. Enter confirms the shot's attempts and opens the next shot.

An attempt is missing: go to its rim frame and press A. An attempt is not a shot: select it and press ⌫. No attempt in the whole shot: empty the list and press Enter.

What counts. Every try at the basket in live play: jump shots, layups, dunks, tip-ins, and also blocked shots and airballs (both are missed). Free throws are left out.
Rim frame: the ball touches the rim or the backboard, or passes through or past the ring. For a blocked shot it is the frame of the block.
Release frame: the first frame with the ball out of the shooter's hands. For a dunk, use the rim frame for both.
Shooter: the player who sends the ball at the basket. On an alley-oop or a tip-in that is the finisher, not the passer.
3 points: both feet behind the three-point line at take-off.

Tab 2 · Ball holder: your call (the frames the first pass could not settle)

One frame a second (every 3 s in full-game shots). The big picture is the frame to label; the row below shows 0.6 s before and after it.

  1. Play the neighbouring frames first (Space, or ↑ ↓). A single frame overstates holds: a ball next to a hand is often a pass on its way.
  2. Holder: click the player who holds the ball (green box). T switches the team if the label on the box is wrong. No box on the holder: drag one around the whole player.
  3. Ball: the orange ring must sit on the ball. If it does not, hold Shift and click the ball (or press B, then click). Ball not in view at all: X.
  4. Enter saves what the picture shows and opens the next frame. N saves "nobody holds it" in one key.

The yellow note on the right is what the first pass saw and why it was not sure. Box numbers in a note are the first pass's own and do not show here. A dashed yellow box or ring is the first pass's proposal where it differs from what is shown.

Who is the holder. Whoever holds or dribbles the ball. A dribbler holds it also while the ball is between hand and floor. An inbounder holding the ball counts. A shooter counts until the ball leaves the hands.
Nobody: the ball is in flight (pass or shot), loose, being tipped or fought for, or in a referee's hands.
Catching: the receiver holds it from the frame the ball is under control, not while it arrives.

Tab 3 · Ball holder: first pass (a second look)

Frames the first pass settled with the neighbouring frames in view. Same keys. Enter confirms a frame as shown; change anything you disagree with first. These count as gold once you have confirmed them.
