Court keyframes
Drag a dot onto the spot where that court point really is; it turns green and the lines re-fit. Shift-click anywhere on a painted line or circle (sideline, centre line, centre circle, three-point arc, key) to pin it: a cyan diamond attaches to the nearest court line, shown in cyan and named; press Tab for the next-nearest line, or pick any line in the "pin on" list at the top (the model's guess can be at the wrong end of the court). Each pin counts half a dot, so a frame without corners in view can still be fitted: put 2 pins on every straight line in view and 3 on every circle or arc. The top bar shows how far the lines could still move (±px); if it says "not pinned down", add pins on lines away from the others. With enough dots and pins the court is fitted to them only. If they cannot fit one court, the dot stays where you put it and the lines keep the last good fit until you fix it.
