Court keyframes
Drag a dot onto the spot where that court point really is; it turns green and the lines re-fit. With 4 or more green dots the court is fitted to the green dots only, so use well-spread points (corners of the key, sideline/baseline corners, the centre line). Put a dot on its exact spot only where you can see it; leave the others alone.
S save and next · ←/→ previous/next · U undo (steps back through every change) · right-click a green dot to release it · R reset to the model's guess · X no usable court (close-up, replay) and next · L hide/show lines · H hide/show dots
