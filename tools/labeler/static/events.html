How to verify (takes ~2 min for all 9 dev shots)
  1. Play or scrub around each shot's rim_frame (green/red diamond on timeline). Compare the Scorebug Start vs End strip if the net is obstructed.
  2. Confirm Made (M) vs Missed (X) and 2 pt (2) vs 3 pt (3).
  3. Click Go to Release, then click the shooter's box on the video (or pick them in the Shooter dropdown).
  4. Delete any false positive, or scrub to a missing shot's rim contact and click + Add shot (A).
  5. Hit ✓ Verified & Next (Enter). Everything autosaves to data/gold/v1/events.json.
