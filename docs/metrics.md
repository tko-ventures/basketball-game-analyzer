# Metrics

Gold-set scores from `bga eval` (plan section 8). One row per config and split, appended by `bga eval --write`; never edited by hand except for this header.

How to read a row:
- **config:** the config's name and the hash of its layer hashes. The same committed config gives the same hash on every split.
- **gold:** the gold version. **v0 is the implementer's labels**, prefilled by the same public models the baseline uses, so v0 scores are biased upwards; use them to compare rows. The v0 track teams were proposed by the same kit classifier, then checked by eye on 2026-11-20 with no classifier involved: all 164 are right, so team accuracy on v0 is a real score. See `docs/gold.md`, team audit.
- **split:** dev is the 9 non-held-out shots. Held-out is the Dziki – Zastal game, scored once per milestone.
- **HOTA, DetA, AssA, IDF1, MOTA, IDSW:** TrackEval over all people (players and referees as one class), on every 5th frame.
- **AP50:** class-agnostic person AP on the 100 box-gold frames. AP50:95 waits for gold v1 geometry.
- **team acc:** gold player tracks whose matched detections are mostly given the right team. The track count is in brackets.
- **number acc:** gold tracks with a legible number whose best-matched predicted track got that number confirmed. In brackets: the fraction that got any number confirmed, the count confirmed wrong, and the number of tracks.
- **ms/frame:** mean time per processed frame on the M1 Max, per layer. Numbers is the jersey-number reader.
- **IDSW of link rows before 192c1f4** is too high: the link layer reordered the rows within a frame, and matching ties follow row order. Re-scored at 192c1f4 with nothing else changed: `link-botsort-reid-tuned` 68 (rows say 73), `-noapp` 67 (72), the `-numbers`, `-owner`, `-roster` and `-roster-all` configs 68 (73), `-roster-all-nojoin` 70 (75, the tracker's own 70), `link-bytetrack-tuned` 108 (112) and `-noapp` 107 (111). HOTA, IDF1 and number accuracy do not change. The `split_cost` 0.4 row (@2055fe80) was not re-scored.

Unmapped keyframes count as failures and have no error.

## Appearance layer

Scored by `bga eval-appearance` on the clean gold boxes of gold v0 (visibility 1: neither interpolated nor occluded), dev shots unless `--held-out`, so the scores depend on neither the detector nor the tracker. The config's appearance backend embeds every box, and candidates are ranked by cosine distance. **1 s, 3 s:** one crop of a player, queried every 0.5 s, against the crops of his teammates 1 s or 3 s later in the same shot. **cross-shot:** the mean embedding of a numbered player in one shot against those of his numbered teammates in another shot of the same game. **chance:** the mean rank-1 of a random ranking. **ms/crop:** the encoder on the M1 Max, crops of one frame in one batch.
