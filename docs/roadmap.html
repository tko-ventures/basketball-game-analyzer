basketball-game-analyzer: roadmap

The goal: from a PLK broadcast video of a Dziki game, know who is where on the court, for every frame. That means finding the players, telling the teams apart, following each player without mixing them up, reading their jersey numbers, and drawing everyone on a 2D FIBA court.

A summary of docs/PLAN.md (sections 2, 5, 8, 9 and 12) and docs/decisions.md. Status as of 2026-11-02.

M0 · Repository bootstrap

✓ Done · ⏳ merge pending #1 · PR #11
Why
A clean start that copies no data from the old prototype, so every result can be reproduced from the repo alone.
What
Imported the useful prototype code (labeller, shot detector, rosters), re-downloaded all 31 clips, regenerated the shot files.
Done when
The labeller opens every clip with its shots ready. Met.

M1 · Gold set

✓ Done (v0) · in review #2 · PR #12
Why
Without a fixed answer key, nobody can tell whether a change helps. The old prototype piled up heuristics it never measured, so this plan measures before it changes anything.
What
12 shots (4 games, 3 seasons, 4 halls), every 5th frame: every player and referee with a persistent identity, team and number. Plus 100 frames where every person is boxed.
Done when
The gold set is committed, with one game held out.
Decision 2026-11-02: I labelled all of it (gold v0) so work can move fast. If the client is interested, your review turns it into v1, and every score is recomputed with one command. v0 scores run high because v0 was built from the same public models the baseline uses.

M2 · Walking skeleton and baseline

✓ Done · in review #3 · branch feat/m2-baseline
Why
Run Skalski's published stack end to end, unchanged, and score it. That gives the number every later change has to beat, and the framework to compare approaches.
What
Swappable layers chosen in a config file, bga run and bga eval, metrics written to docs/metrics.md, radar videos, and a runner that sends GPU work to Modal.
Done when
The first full metrics table exists, citing baseline.yaml.
First row in docs/metrics.md: HOTA 0.60 on dev, 0.65 on held-out, AP50 0.91, numbers right on 48% of tracks. Tracking breaks people into 2 to 4 times too many identities (that is M6), and in Sopot a third of positions land off the court (that is M3).

M3 · Court layer

○ Next #4
Why
The NBA court model works in Warsaw but misplaces lines in Lublin and mostly fails in Sopot, and every 2D position depends on it. This is the biggest visible gap for the client.
What
Label court landmarks on frames from every PLK hall, retrain the court model on a rented GPU, and smooth it within each shot. This is also the first training run, so it brings training configs and the leakage test.
Done when
The reprojection error is reported per arena. Provisional target: under 0.5 m near the paint.

M4 · Detection fine-tune

○ Later #5
Why
The detector learned on NBA frames. PLK has different kits, sponsor logos, floors and camera angles, so it misses people (players on the floor, referees at the frame edge).
What
Auto-label PLK frames, review them, and train RF-DETR on them. Compare against two other open models trained the same way. Export it to run on the Mac.
Done when
Per-class accuracy and speed per frame are reported.

M5 · Team layer

○ Later #6
Why
Telling the teams apart by kit colour fails in specific cases: light leggings under a dark kit, the white score overlay, fans in a team colour.
What
Skalski's kit-colour classifier per clip, plus a check that warns when the two teams' colours are too close to separate.
Done when
Team accuracy is reported. Provisional target: 98%.

M6 · Tracking and linking

○ Later #7
Why
“No identity confusion” is the core requirement. Simple trackers break a player into many pieces and swap identities when players cross.
What
Track in court metres instead of pixels, then link the pieces offline using team, number and the rule of 5 players per team on the floor. Also compare against SAM 2 tracking, which ran on a rented GPU for the gold-set prefill.
Done when
HOTA and IDF1 are reported for both trackers, before and after linking.

M7 · Numbers and identity

○ Later #8
Why
A jersey number plus the roster gives a player's name. Numbers are small and blurred in the highlights, so the readers need PLK training and a fair comparison.
What
Label about 300 number crops (on better footage if the club has full games). Compare a small classifier with several vision-language models, limited to the numbers on that team's roster.
Done when
Identity accuracy on the held-out game is reported.

M8 · 2D positions

○ Later #9
Why
The bottom of a player's box is a poor estimate of where the feet touch the floor (jumps, the frame edge). Ankles from pose estimation are better.
What
Ground points from the ankles, handling of jumps, the final export format (one row per player per frame), and a radar video.
Done when
Positions are exported for every gold shot and checked visually.

M9 · Ball, possession, shots

○ Last #10
Why
Possession and shot events turn positions into basketball analysis. It comes last because it builds on every layer above.
What
Ball detection (tiled if it is too small to find), the holder taken from the player in possession, and made and missed shots.
Done when
Possession accuracy on the gold shots is reported.

Rules that shape every step

Measure first

No change goes into a layer without a before/after row against the gold set. Done is a number, not an opinion.

Swappable layers

A new approach is added beside the old one and selected in a config. The two are compared on the same shots, never by editing the pipeline.

Follow Skalski

Start from Roboflow's published basketball pipeline. Deviate only where his posts name a limitation or where PLK differs from the NBA.

Open models, trained on PLK

Production uses open-weight models fine-tuned on PLK frames. Closed AI services are used only to help with labelling.

Mac first, GPU rented

Inference runs on the M1 Max. Training and GPU-only models run on Modal, capped at a proposed 50 USD a month (3.60 USD spent so far).

Honest test

No gold frame is ever used for training. The held-out game is scored once per milestone.
